# Designing and Printing a PCB

**Course:** EC EN 340 · **Term:** Fall 2026 · **Type:** Design project
**Tools:** KiCad, NGSpice

> **One-line summary:** "Organized components on a schematic, and designed physical board in KiCad to be printed at JLC."

## Context
Our final receiver/transmitter board is a PCB made of through-hole components. We had to design the circuitry, and organization of these components on the board.

## My contribution
I spent time learning the basics of KiCad, to first layout the schematic in its final version, create label names and values, and choose footprints. 
After, I designed the traces on the board, carefully placing components to allow for short, straight connections and easy pin access. All labels were obvious, avoiding drill holes, and the board grounded on both sides.
