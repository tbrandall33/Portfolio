# Soldering a Transmitter board

**Course:** EC EN 340 · **Term:** Fall 2026 · **Type:** Design project
**Tools:** Soldering iron, multimeter, solder oven, solder paste

> **One-line summary:** "Soldered pins of a small transmitter board using solder paste and a solder oven. Tested voltages and connections with a multimeter. "

## Context
For right now, the transmitter board is used to direct current through an LED intended to simulate 'shooting' other players in the Laser Tag project. 

## My contribution
I spent some time layering the tiny footprints of the transmitter board with solder paste using a toothpick, after which I used a soldering oven for a few minutes to finalize things.
Afterwards, I checked continuity between every combination of pins, to check for any bad soldering and connection issues that may cause problems in the future.
More soldering and testing will be performed within the week on the rest of the PCB board since it's now printed. Results will be reported here.

## Result
[Transmitter board](transmitter_board.png)
The resulting transmitter board was successful in it's functionality and a sufficient amount of current was measured through the LED. 


## What went wrong, and what I changed
Originally when measuring the current through the diode, we were getting poor results. We simply had to switch out our LED for better results. Our original diode wasn't performing as well.
